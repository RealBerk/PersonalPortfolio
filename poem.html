The Gathering at Eden

Şiir · 2026

Tarafımdan yazılmış şiir

As the world burns,
Every fool is armed with gasoline,
Each pore overflown with petrol.
Madmen coated in philosophies, bent and keen,
A soup of innocence boils and churns,
An orgy of wildfire, our joy as mere coal.
Whom shall save our souls?

Design remains a deluge of folly,
Pygmies cursed with hindsight ne`er do more than dawdle and scribble.
Unwilling turncoats abandon their banner and flee,
The one who yet not die remain a cripple.
Burdened by fantasies of smoke and glee,
Consequences of passion hollow cause each thread rupture and ripple.
Embers consume families to tear alas no ear left to hear.

Dance of nations, the march to love death,
Leaves all blind to flail around and harm beloved.
Castles of sand isolate each until the odd wind or wave,
The crowd self-mutilates in frenzy and a rush so crowded,
War drums roll, horns wail and a sibling slays another in wrath.
Lunatics howl at the moon, mouths foam and so gathers the rave,
Magnificence of creation, seeking pilgrims, and mindless cretins,
Who can possible escape blame?
